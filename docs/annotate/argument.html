Argument annotation

Mark where each passage breaks into parts, then name each part. Click the space before a word to put a break there; click again to take it away. Mark breaks where the argument moves to a new claim, as you would with a pen. Then choose a role for each part. Work on your own: the lens's answers are not shown here.

Roles: premise (a claim the argument starts from), minor premise (the second claim that applies the first, often after "but", "now", "sed", "ora", "or", "ahora bien"), supporting premise (a reason for another premise), assumption (a condition or presupposition taken for granted), conclusion (where the argument lands), turn (a move against what came before), other.
