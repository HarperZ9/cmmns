Radar options

Choose where Radar runs, what its window shows and how long it keeps what it has read. Changes apply at once.

Sites

Mastodon servers

Radar runs on a Mastodon server only after you open it, click the Radar button and allow it. Removing a server here takes the access back.

    What the window shows

    Reading

    Site cards

    Rest the pointer on a link in a post to see who owns the site, what kind of owner it has, when and where it was founded, any state link, and its fact-check record. To see the same card for a news site you are reading, press Site card for this page in Radar's toolbar menu. Radar runs on no news site until you press it.

    Search results

    Off by default. Turning an engine on asks the browser for access to its result pages; Radar then adds a line of labels under each result and sends nothing anywhere.

    Other coverage

    Data packs

    A data pack adds fact-checks, operation indicators or site notes from someone you choose. Radar never downloads one: you pick the file. Radar checks its signature, shows which key signed it and what it adds, and uses it only after you trust that key.

        What Radar keeps

        Everything stays in this browser profile. Older entries are deleted on the next start and whenever you change these numbers.

        Accessibility

        Keyboard: move focus to a post to open its window, Alt+R to move into it, arrow keys between its tabs, Esc to close it.

        Your feedback on signs

        Every sign in the window has This was right and This was wrong. Your marks stay in this browser. Radar keeps the sign, its kind, the thread it was in and your mark. It keeps the post text only when you tick "include the text" before marking.

        SignRightWrong

        Saving writes a JSON file to your computer. Radar never sends it. If you want to help measure Radar, you can attach the file to an issue on Radar's repository. Read it first: it holds the threads you marked, and the text of any post you chose to include.

        Evidence, not verdicts. Nothing leaves this browser unless you turn on Other coverage.
