Daylight Commons

See the evidence behind the replies you read. Rest the pointer on a reply and Daylight Commons shows where the platform places the account, signs of coordination, and how the thread's harshest replies compare with the rest.

What Daylight Commons shows

  • Where an account is based, as X labels it, beside any reply that speaks as a local of somewhere else.
  • Signs of coordination: the same text from several accounts, the same upload pushed by many, bursts of replies.
  • How an account posts: its age, renames, posting hours and the apps it posts from.
  • A thread report: whether the most extreme replies come mostly from flagged accounts, with an interval.

What Daylight Commons never does

  • No verdicts. It never calls anyone a bot, a propagandist or an extremist. Every sign says what it does not prove.
  • Nothing leaves your browser unless you turn on Other coverage, which says which outlets it asks before you do and sends nothing from the page. No server, no account, no analytics. What Daylight Commons keeps stays in this browser profile.
  • No guessing who someone is. No names, employers, homes or demographics, and no linking accounts across sites.
  • Political side is never an input. The same words score the same whoever they target.

Where Daylight Commons runs

Switch off any site you do not want it on. You can change this later in Daylight Commons' options.

How sure it is

Each sign shows its measured precision, or "not measured yet" where no measurement exists.

More options
Keyboard: move focus to a post to open its window, Alt+R to move into it, Esc to close it.
