Daylight Commons

See the evidence behind the replies you read. Rest the pointer on a reply and Daylight Commons shows where the platform places the account, signs of coordination, and how the thread's harshest replies compare with the rest.

What Daylight Commons shows

  • Where an account is based, as X labels it, beside any reply that speaks as a local of somewhere else.
  • Signs of coordination: the same text from several accounts, the same upload pushed by many, bursts of replies.
  • How an account posts: its age, renames, posting hours and the apps it posts from.
  • A thread report: whether the most extreme replies come mostly from flagged accounts, with an interval.

How Daylight Commons works

  • Signs you can check. Each sign names the words or pattern it matched and how to read it, so you judge the account yourself.
  • Runs in your browser. Daylight Commons reads the site you are on, and what it keeps stays in this browser profile. Other coverage and evidence lookups reach further once you turn them on and click, and each shows what it sends and to whom first.
  • About posts, kept apart from identities. Daylight Commons describes what an account posts and how. It leaves names, employers, homes and demographics alone, and keeps each site's accounts separate.
  • Same words, same score. Scores come from the words alone, so they stay the same whoever the words target.

Where Daylight Commons runs

Keep the sites you want it on. You can change this later in Daylight Commons' options.

How sure it is

Each sign shows its measured precision once it has one.
